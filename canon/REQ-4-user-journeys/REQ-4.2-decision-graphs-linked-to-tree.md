# Journeys are decision graphs whose steps link many-to-many to requirements at any level

## Stated

> Journeys are decision graphs whose steps link many-to-many to requirements at any level

Source: docs/design-brief-v0.md:66

## Intent [assumed]

Journeys branch and loop. A login step may link to many requirements; a performance requirement may link to many steps. The tree is not built from journeys.
