# Three target failure modes: substitution, justification, erosion

## Stated

> Three target failure modes: substitution, justification, erosion

Source: docs/design-brief-v0.md:53

## Intent [assumed]

Substitution: the agent reinterprets the request to fit something easier. Justification: it reads the evidence so what shipped looks like what was asked. Erosion: it fits the current context but overwrites older goals without asking.

## Not this [assumed]

Catching ordinary bugs. These are failures of alignment, not of correctness.
