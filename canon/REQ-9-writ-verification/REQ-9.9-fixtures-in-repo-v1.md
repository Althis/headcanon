# Small datasets are committed to the repo as fixtures in v1; the canon records each source; the user curates ground truth

## Stated

> Small datasets are committed to the repo as fixtures in v1; the canon records each source; the user curates ground truth

Source: docs/design-brief-v0.md:94

## Assumptions

- Datasets are versioned and linked to requirements, like tests. (model assumption)
- Changing a dataset or its ground truth counts as a test change and needs the same approval. (model assumption)
- Test data is synthetic or anonymized; production personal data is never used. (model assumption)
